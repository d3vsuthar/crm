01 · Models

Targeting, segmentation and customer models

Predictive models I've built and deployed in production. They power targeting and personalisation in live campaigns every week.

Recommendation engine

Predicts which brands a customer is likely to buy next. Used for product recommendations in campaigns and for launch audiences.

Channel engagement scores

Predicts how likely each customer is to engage on email and push. Used to pick the lead channel and to reduce sends to low-engagement customers, which protects deliverability.

Customer segmentation & VIP tiers

Segments customers by value and shopping behaviour, with different messaging and offers per segment. Also used for VIP and private-sale lists.

Profile enrichment

Predicts missing profile attributes, which reduces the number of customers who get generic messages.

03 · Operations

Automating campaign production

Campaigns across multiple markets, channels and languages took a lot of manual work. Each one was built by hand for every market and language, which was slow and error-prone.

What's automated

  • Plan to draft. Drafts are created from the campaign calendar.
  • Email building. Emails are assembled from approved templates, in multiple languages.
  • Daily checks. Upcoming campaigns are checked for missing content, broken links and stock.
  • Test sends. Proofs go to the right test lists.
  • Reporting. Results are written back to the campaign calendar.

Built-in safety

Automation prepares and checks campaigns. A person approves and sends.

04 · Measurement

Measuring incremental impact from CRM activities

Opens and clicks measure engagement, not revenue, and each platform took credit for the same orders. That made it hard to tell how much revenue CRM drove. This work measures the incremental revenue from CRM.

Control groups

Journeys and campaigns are compared against random holdout groups.

Experiment design

Each test has a hypothesis, a primary metric and a fixed duration, agreed before launch.
